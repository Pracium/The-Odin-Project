Scrambled Eggs with Toast

Description

Scrambled Eggs with Toast are a great and healthy breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 2-3 eggs
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tsp butter or oil
  • 2 slices of bread

Steps

  1. Crack eggs into a bowl, add a pinch of salt and pepper, and whisk.
  2. Heat butter or oil in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour in eggs and stir gently until cooked through but still soft.
  4. Toast bread.
  5. Server eggs on the side or on top of toast.
