Chicken Sandwich

Ingredients

  • 1 cooked chicken breast
  • 2 slices of bread
  • 1-2 tsp mayonnaise or butter
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Slice the cooked chicken into thin pieces.
  2. Spread mayonnaise or butter on the bread.
  3. Place chicken slices on one slice of bread, season with salt and pepper.
  4. Top with the other slice of bread.
  5. Cut in half and serve.
