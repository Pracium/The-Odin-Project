Pasta with Tomato Sauce

Ingredients

  • 100-150g pasta
  • 1 cup tomato sauce (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Boil pasta in salted water according to package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and add tomato sauce. Simmer for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Drain pasta and add it to the sauce. Mix well.
  4. Serve with optional grated cheese on top.
